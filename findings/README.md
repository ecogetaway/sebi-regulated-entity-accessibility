## Why the baseline is time-critical

SEBI's audit and remediation deadline is **31 October 2026**. The research question this
repository can answer — *are the requirements translating into observable improvements?* —
needs a before and an after.

The August run is the only record of "before" that will ever exist. Pages change; a
re-scan in November measures November. The baseline must therefore be verified and frozen
**before 31 October**, not after.
