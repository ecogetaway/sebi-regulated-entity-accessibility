# Roadmap

## Now — before 31 October 2026

The audit and remediation deadline is 31 October. The August baseline is the only record of
"before" that will ever exist, and it decays: pages change, and a re-scan in November
measures November. Everything in this phase is time-boxed by that.

- [ ] **Resolve [OQ-1](regulation/open-questions.md)** — has SEBI amended its model Investor
      Charter? Criterion S3 stays held until it is settled. Requires access to
      `sebi.gov.in`.
- [ ] **Resolve [OQ-3](regulation/open-questions.md)** — read the 23 May 2025 KYC circular
      (SEBI/HO/MIRSD/SECFATF/P/CIR/2025/74) against primary text. The only one of the six
      not yet read in full.
- [ ] **Hand-verify the August baseline** and freeze it. Every failure reproduced,
      evidenced, dated.
- [ ] **Publish the first entity profiles** from the verified baseline, with right of reply
      offered before publication.
- [ ] Run the journey protocol, steps 2–10, on accounts held.

## After 31 October 2026

- [ ] Re-run the harness against the identical surface list.
- [ ] Publish the comparison: did the deadline produce observable change?
- [ ] Re-assess whether an Overall Accessibility Benchmark derived from Scores A and B is

## Wanted

- **A tester who uses a screen reader daily.** No person with a disability has yet run any
  step of the journey protocol. Until that changes, dimension 4 is an informed simulation
  and is labelled as one. This is the single biggest weakness in the method.
- **Mobile app assessment.** Every major broker ships native iOS and Android apps that no
  web scanner reaches. Out of scope in v0.1; needs devices and a tester.
- **SCORES accessibility complaint data.** The category exists and the data is being
  collected. It is not published. Worth an RTI request.
