# Corrections

Errors found in material this repository has already published, with what was wrong, what
it now says, and where it was fixed. Corrections are made in place and recorded here, so
anyone who read an earlier version can see what changed.

## 10 October 2026 — the obligation count

**What was wrong.** The summary under the register in
[`regulation/obligations.md`](regulation/obligations.md) said the 24 obligations split into
**7 public, 4 testable on the platform, 2 needing an account and 11 not observable**, and
that "nine of those eleven" would become observable if an entity published them. That
summary did not match the rows of the register itself. The claim log
([`evidence/claim-verification.md`](evidence/claim-verification.md), row R18) had marked it
verified without recounting the rows.

**What it now says.** Counted row by row: **5 public, 3 testable on the platform, 2 needing an
account, 14 not observable.** Seven of the fourteen (O2, O4, O12, O13, O14, O16, O23) become
partially observable if an entity chooses to publish. O22 is split (signposting public,
resolution not) and is counted as public. The classification of each row is unchanged.

**Where it was fixed.** `regulation/obligations.md`, `problem-definition.md`,
`methodology/what-we-cannot-see.md`, `rubric/rubric.md`, `guide/investor-guide.md`,
`guide/Investor_Accessibility_Guide_v1.docx`, `evidence/claim-verification.md` and
`.xlsx` (R18 now marked corrected).

**Effect on the findings.** None on any score. The corrected count strengthens the finding
it supports: more than half the obligations cannot be checked from outside.

## 10 October 2026 — "eleven brokers" left in three files

The August baseline checked **ten** brokers' Investor Charters; the eleventh, 5paisa, blocks
automated retrieval. This was corrected in August (claim log row C3) but three files still
said eleven: `regulation/open-questions.md`, `rubric/rubric.md` (the S3 hold) and
`methodology/test-journey.md`. All three now say ten, with 5paisa recorded as unmeasured.
