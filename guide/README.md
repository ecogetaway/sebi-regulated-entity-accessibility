# Investor guide

**[Can You Actually Use Your Broker's App?](Investor_Accessibility_Guide_v1.docx)** — a
plain-language guide for investors to a right most of them do not know they have.

23 pages. No technical background assumed. It explains what digital accessibility means,
where the right comes from, what SEBI required of every Regulated Entity, why none of that
is externally verifiable — and then the part that is actually actionable: six checks any
investor can run in twenty minutes, and how to escalate to a SCORES accessibility complaint,
which cannot be closed until the barrier is fixed.

[`investor-guide.md`](investor-guide.md) is the source. Corrections welcome by issue or
pull request; if you find something that is wrong, or a step that does not work in practice,
that is the most useful thing you can send.

## Why this is in the repository

The rest of this project measures. This is the other half.

SEBI's circulars created an entitlement and no public accountability surface — every
compliance filing is private, so an investor cannot compare firms or check whether their
own broker has done the work. The one mechanism that does reach an investor is the
Accessibility complaint category on SCORES, and it only works if people know it exists.

Research that identifies a gap and then keeps the remedy in a research format has not
finished the job.
