# The SEBI digital accessibility circulars

Six documents, May 2025 – July 2026. Every date and quotation in this repository is taken
from the circular text itself, not from secondary coverage.

therefore read as the **exact SEBI PDFs redistributed by regulated market bodies** — APMI,
MUFG Intime, NSDL and BSE. Those are SEBI's own documents, republished by entities under

**The 14 December 2025 milestone was replaced, not supplemented.** Circular 5 says:
"Instead of meeting the compliance requirement for appointment of accessibility auditor by
December 14, 2025, REs shall submit a status of their readiness and compliance to the
accessibility requirements for each of their digital platforms latest by March 31, 2026."

Several published guides — including the most complete one in the field — still list both
as sequential live milestones. They are not.

## Standards, verbatim

Circular 2 Annexure I §B names four things as "the baseline for compliance":

1. "Web Content Accessibility Guidelines ("WCAG") 2.1 or latest version."
2. Accessibility guidelines in the latest version of GIGW.
3. IS 17802, Indian Standards on Accessibility Requirements for ICT Products and Services.
4. The RPwD Act 2016 provisions cited in the circular.

**SEBI does not write "WCAG 2.2" anywhere, does not write "GIGW 3.0", and gives IS 17802 no
year.** Coverage that cites "WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA", "GIGW 3.0" or "IS 17802:2021" is
extrapolating from "or latest version". This repository cites what the circular says.

SEBI also does not name PDF/UA. Annexure I §2.2 points instead at the WCAG PDF techniques
at `w3.org/TR/WCAG20-TECHS/pdf`.

## Legal basis

Issued under s.11(1) of the SEBI Act 1992, enforcing ss. 40, 42 and 46 of the RPwD Act 2016
and Rule 15(1)(c) of the RPwD Rules 2017.

The origin is the Supreme Court's judgment of **30 April 2025 in *Pragya Prasun & Ors. v.
Union of India*, heard with *Amar Jain v. Union of India***, which held that the right to
digital access is an intrinsic component of the right to life and personal liberty under
Article 21. Circular 2 cites it directly.

**The circulars prescribe no penalty.** They are issued under the general s.11(1) power.
Coverage asserting a specific penalty regime — daily fines, registration suspension — is
describing SEBI's general enforcement powers, not anything in these circulars.

## Compliance is reported privately

Every submission goes by **email** to `digital_acc@sebi.gov.in`, or to the entity's stock
exchange, depository, or BSE Ltd., per Annexure A of circular 5. There is no public
register, no published audit report, and no VPAT requirement.

This single fact determines the entire method of this repository. See
[`../methodology/what-we-cannot-see.md`](../methodology/what-we-cannot-see.md).
